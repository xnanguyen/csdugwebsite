# Resource Links

The resource section is organized into Academics, Career, Research, and Opportunities, with six links per group. URLs live in `assets/data/site-content.json`; labels and grouping live in `index.html`.

The starting list came from the user's [shared resource-planning conversation](https://chatgpt.com/share/6ab39a8c-2920-83ea-8cff-75b34583c9f6). Destinations were reviewed on September 23, 2026 against Brown's own pages, not treated as verified just because the chat cited them.

## Destination Choices

- The [CS course catalog](https://cs.brown.edu/courses/) includes links to sections and current schedules. The chat's course-catalog citation mistakenly pointed to career planning.
- The [CS career planning page](https://cs.brown.edu/degrees/career-planning/) links both course-to-career and job-title-to-course maps.
- The [CS contact page](https://cs.brown.edu/about/contacts/) contains the Gigs mailing-list signup link. It is used instead of an unverified mailing-list endpoint.
- The old industry-partner landing page returned 403 and its current-partners page returned 404 during review. The public [Career Center events page](https://career-center.brown.edu/connections-opportunities/events) and [BrownConnect+](https://brownconnectplus.brown.edu/) provide working event/network alternatives.
- [Job search resources](https://career-center.brown.edu/planning-resources/job-search-resources) links resume, cover-letter, and interview guidance.
- [TA hiring](https://cs.brown.edu/courses/ta/hiring/) is the direct hiring page linked by Brown's UTA overview.
- [UTRA](https://utra.brown.edu/) is linked directly for funding. The [undergraduate research guide](https://cs.brown.edu/degrees/undergrad/research/) also links NSF REU opportunities.
- The separate student-groups directory redirects to a login. The public [undergraduate resources page](https://cs.brown.edu/degrees/undergrad/resources/) lists student organizations and general resources instead.
- No application-open badges, deadlines, or promises of available research positions are shown; those change and should be checked at their official sources.

Some linked services (including alumni networking and applications) can require a Brown account after following the public landing page. This site does not collect credentials or submit applications.
